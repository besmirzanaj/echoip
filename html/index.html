FAQ

        

          How do I force IPv4 or IPv6 lookup?
          

            Pass the flag to your client: curl -4 or
            curl -6. The v4/v6 subdomains were
            retired in 2018.
          

        

        

          Can I force getting JSON?
          
Yes — send an Accept: application/json header.

        

        

          Is automated use of this service permitted?
          

            Yes, at up to 1 request per minute. Requests beyond that may be
            rate-limited with a 429 status code, or dropped entirely.
